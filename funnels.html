Supply funnel

Ad requests on the deal's ad units, through the initial validation and the policy, consent and IVT checks, to valid opportunities for demand. Then the impressions that reached deal matching for this deal and passed it.

Phase A counts belong to the deal's ad units and are shared with every other deal on them. Do not add them up across deals.

Demand funnel

DSP

Starts where the supply funnel ends: impressions that passed deal matching. Then the bid requests sent to the deal's DSPs, their bid responses, HX auction wins and rendered impressions.

Phase C counts are per DSP: an impression sent to several DSPs is one bid request for each of them.
